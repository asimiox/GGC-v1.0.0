# Brag Plan: GGC M.B.Din Official App

## What is this app?
The official mobile application for Govt. Graduate College Mandi Bahauddin, serving thousands of students and faculty with authentic real-time academic portals, announcements, admissions, and departmental administration.

## The angle
A dignified, pristine, and modern institutional showcase. Not a flashy tech startup ad, but a credible, authentic collegiate platform presentation highlighting actual interfaces that students and faculty use everyday.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The prestigious GGC Mandi Bahauddin emblem and gold-accented insignia emerging on deep royal navy, establishing authenticity and official status immediately.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Official Splash & Onboarding**: Clean portal selection for Intermediate & BS 4-Year degree programs.
- **Student & Faculty Home**: Live student dashboard, attendance, timetable, notices, and academic tools.
- **Academic Programs Showcase**: Computer Science, Physics, English, Chemistry, and departmental curricula.
- **Modern Announcements Portal**: The newly redesigned minimal announcements creator and official college bulletin board.
- **Administrative & Faculty Hub**: Verified single-device secure access and departmental management.

## Outro / punchline
"Govt. Graduate College Mandi Bahauddin — Your Campus in Your Pocket." Clean transition into official collegiate crest, download prompt, and verified official app badge.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "Official, modern, clean, trustworthy, educational college portal showcase."
- Format: Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920)
- Duration: 20 seconds
- Audio: Ende "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol 1", subtle professional background bed at balanced volume.
